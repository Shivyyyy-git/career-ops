## Role Overview

Generalist Product Manager seat that flexes across multiple product areas: consumer AI products, enterprise platforms, developer tools, speech and voice systems, or edge deployments. High-ownership, ship-every-week culture. No layers between PM, engineering, design, and leadership.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own a product area end-to-end: talk to users, write specs, define success metrics, ship, and measure outcomes.
- Analyze AI system behavior in production: prompt design, model selection, latency constraints, and failure modes.
- Write code, prompts, and copy when needed for rapid learning and validation.
- Establish funnels and instrument events using analytics tools (PostHog, Mixpanel) for diagnostics.
- Work directly with engineering, design, and leadership. No layers. No hand-offs.
- Ship product every week, not plans.

## Requirements

**Must-Have:**
- 3 to 7 years of product management experience with proven shipping at scale in strong B2C or B2B Indian companies.
- Currently employed as a product manager (not transitioning from consulting or program management).
- Hands-on AI experience: agent building, context window understanding, cost-latency-accuracy tradeoffs.
- SQL proficiency and data analytics tool expertise (PostHog, Mixpanel).
- User-centric product thinking with ability to structure ambiguous problems.
- Systems thinking regarding component interactions and dependencies.
- High ownership and autonomy mindset.
- Clear communication of technical concepts to diverse audiences.

**Nice-to-Have:**
- Built end-to-end AI agents beyond demos.
- Experience with Claude Code, skills development, or agentic workflows.
- Side projects, open-source contributions, or shipped personal products.
- Demonstrated deep impact in 2 to 3 significant personal or professional initiatives.
