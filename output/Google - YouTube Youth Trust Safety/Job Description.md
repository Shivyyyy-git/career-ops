## About the Job

Unique opportunity to redefine how creators interact with YouTube's policies using AI. Partner with Engineering and Youth Trust and Safety cross-functional teams to transform the policy experience from a friction point into a learning partnership, leveraging AI to explain policy violations and help creators fix their content. Design scalable, automated solutions that enable safe expansion of creator tools, at the intersection of safety and creator empowerment, maintaining a healthy ecosystem while reducing reliance on manual operations.

## Responsibilities

- Drive the development of AI-powered solutions that proactively identify potential violations, explain policies clearly, and guide creators through automated remediation workflows
- Enable safe expansion of creator tools to broader audiences by designing abuse-mitigation frameworks that balance risk reduction with ecosystem growth
- Collaborate with Engineering, UX, Data Science, Youth Trust and Safety Operations, GAPP, Public Relations, Legal teams to navigate safety issues and deliver seamless, transparent creator experiences
- Define product goal, strategy, and requirements for creator policy experience products, transforming policy interactions from blockers into educational opportunities that empower creators
