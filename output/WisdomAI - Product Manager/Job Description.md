## About WisdomAI

WisdomAI is on a mission to make working with data delightful. We empower businesses to unlock insights, drive innovation, and make data-driven decisions at scale. We are a fast-paced, early-stage startup, currently ~30 people and growing. (Total raised: $73M; $50M Series A Nov 2025 led by Kleiner Perkins with NVentures / Nvidia VC arm. Founded by Soham Mazumdar, Rubrik co-founder.)

## The Role: A Builder, An Empath, and An Executor

This is not a traditional Product Manager position; it is a dynamic, hands-on function built for the age of AI-native product development. We are a build-first company that values execution and rapid iteration over lengthy documentation.

At WisdomAI, the Product Manager acts as a catalyst for collective intelligence, empowering engineers and designers to propose and build, keenly capture customer feedback, and continuously strive for improvement. This role requires a minimum of 4+ years of relevant experience and a deep balance of technical understanding and customer empathy.

## Core Responsibilities

The PM role serves as the glue that connects the customer's needs, the market's demands, and our team's creative and technical capabilities.

### 1. Customer Empathy and Data Mastery

- Cultivate deep customer empathy by spending time with prospects and users, observing workflows, and understanding their business context.
- Translate customer pain points into clear and actionable problem statements for the development team.
- Leverage your technical and data understanding to evaluate product performance, interpret complex analytics, and inform strategic decisions.
- Proactively track qualitative and quantitative feedback by monitoring support channels, listening to sales calls, and checking in with customer-facing teams.

### 2. Technical Depth and Execution Orientation

- Guide the team in choosing the right development approach for the task at hand (PM-first, Design-first, or Dev-first), relying on your technical understanding of our platform.
- Define the problem and its constraints, allowing engineers to write robust solution proposals.
- Maintain a relentless execution-oriented focus, seeing yourself as an extension of the development team, unblocking them and consistently following through on commitments to drive features to launch.
- Work effectively in our fast-paced environment, balancing immediate user needs with long-term strategic goals.

### 3. Strategic Communication

- Facilitate discussions, lead brainstorms, and articulate a compelling product vision to align the entire organization.
- Act as a product evangelist, collaborating with sales, marketing, and customer success to clearly articulate the product's value.
- Proactively close the feedback loop, ensuring insights from users and the market are constantly flowing back into the development process.

## What You'll Bring: Qualifications

- 4+ years of experience in Product Management, specifically within B2B SaaS, Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, or related AI-native platforms.
- Proven track record of successful product delivery and strategic execution in a fast-paced environment.
- Demonstrated technical and data understanding — must be comfortable discussing APIs, data pipelines, model performance, and architectural trade-offs with customers and engineers.
- Deeply rooted customer empathy and proficiency in interpreting user analytics and business metrics to guide product strategy.
- Strong collaborative and stakeholder management skills across Product, Engineering, Sales, and Marketing.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
