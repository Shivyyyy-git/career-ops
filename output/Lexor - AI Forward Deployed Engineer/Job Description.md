## About the company

Lexor is building AI to systematically dismantle fraud, waste, and abuse in the US government. The product is an agentic AI platform that ingests public and government-restricted data sources (USA Spending, SAM.gov, agency contracting databases, Medicare/Medicaid claims data), reasons across them, and surfaces specific fraud cases with the evidence chain that lets government auditors act. Currently focused on Medicare and Medicaid fraud (~$500B annual addressable problem).

Traction (founder-reported): $200M+ in previously-unidentified fraud found across 3-4 agencies; active conversations at CMS, HHS, Department of War, Homeland Security, state and local governments; one completed funding round.

Founders: Rithik Puli (CEO, CMU BBA, serial founder, DC gov-contracting family network) and Saharsha Navani (CTO, AI research since 2017, Google DeepMind internship in high school, founded and sold Inky Technologies, $2M pre-seed). Both under 22.

## About the role

First cohort of Forward Deployed Engineers (Member of Technical Staff), split across two teams:

- **Team 1 — Black-Box / Top-Secret:** Department of War, Homeland Security. US citizens only (non-negotiable). Pentagon on-site 2-3x/week. TS clearance sponsored.
- **Team 2 — Civilian Agency:** CMS, HHS, state and local. US citizens preferred but not required (O-1 candidates on the table).

Mandate: architect and ship agentic AI systems from proof-of-concept through production; LLM applications (multi-agent frameworks, tool-calling agents, RAG pipelines, human-in-the-loop autonomy); embed with government partners; build data curation and evaluation pipelines; maintain model inference/experimentation/deployment infrastructure; own the full system lifecycle. High-caliber communication with non-technical government stakeholders.
