## Company Overview

Construction is a $13T industry that powers civilization. It is also running on PDFs, rulers, and 1990s software.

The biggest choke point is cost estimates. Takeoffs are manual, brutal, and slow. Estimators spend days counting materials from drawings by hand. One mistake blows millions.

Bobyard is automating construction takeoff with AI.

We use CV plus NLP to read drawings like a senior estimator, but 10x faster with zero errors. What took 3 days now takes 30 minutes. We are turning the most painful part of construction into software.

We are a lean, elite team backed by the best VCs on earth: **8VC, Primary, and Pear**. We are building next-gen software to drag the built world into the future.

## Position Overview

Great products win markets. As we scale our core and launch new 0 to 1 bets, we need a product leader who operates like a founder. You will work directly with the CEO and own the product roadmap end-to-end. You will:

- **Live in customer workflows:** Get in the field with estimators, PMs, and GCs. Understand the job better than they can articulate it.
- **Shape vision and roadmap:** Decide what we build, what we kill, and how we win. Turn market chaos into a product that feels inevitable.
- **Create, iterate, and validate new products:** Own 0 to 1. From napkin to revenue. Test, ship, learn, repeat.
- **Execute with engineering and UI/UX:** No handoffs. You are in the details. Specs, Figma, code reviews, user calls.
- **Communicate vision with marketing and sales:** Make the market believe. Arm GTM to sell what we build and shape the category.
