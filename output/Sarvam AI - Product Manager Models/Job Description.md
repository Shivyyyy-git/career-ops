## Role Summary

Sarvam AI seeks a Model Product Manager to own charters for Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Text-to-Speech (TTS), and model evaluation pipelines, collaborating with research and engineering teams on roadmaps, training cycles, and model releases.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and drive roadmaps for ASR, TTS, and evaluation systems
- Partner with teams on data collection, training cycles, and model release planning
- Set up and own evaluation frameworks and quality metrics covering accuracy, latency, MOS, and robustness
- Run structured experiments including offline tests, A/B tests, and human evaluations
- Ensure scalable deployment and monitoring with infrastructure and data teams
- Translate business needs into measurable model goals
- Communicate priorities and trade-offs across stakeholders

## Requirements

**Must-Have:**
- 3+ years of experience building AI, applied AI, ML products, or model training pipelines
- Familiarity with model lifecycle: training, fine-tuning, deployment, monitoring
- Strong analytical skills and data-driven decision-making capability
- Cross-functional collaboration ability across research, engineering, and product

**Nice-to-Have:**
- Speech technology exposure (ASR, TTS) or evaluation pipelines
- Quality metrics experience (WER, CER, MOS, latency tracking)
- Hands-on familiarity with SQL, Python, or ML toolkits
