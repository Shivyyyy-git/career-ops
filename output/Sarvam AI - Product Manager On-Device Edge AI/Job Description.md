## Role Overview

This seat owns Sarvam's on-device AI product line: enterprise-grade AI that runs on-device, at the edge, and inside customer security perimeters. The role sits at the intersection of fleet management, enterprise IT integration, and on-device model inference.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own the product roadmap for on-device inference, enterprise deployment, and fleet management.
- Define and prioritize features for IT admin personas: MDM integration, device provisioning, policy enforcement, enterprise authentication (Microsoft ecosystem).
- Drive B2B GTM alignment by translating customer pain points into product requirements.
- Run iterative experiments in enterprise deployments to improve workflow adoption and stickiness.
- Design features for multi-tenant SaaS architecture covering tenant isolation, access controls, and admin tooling.
- Define requirements for HITL and agentic workflows ensuring enterprise-grade reliability and auditability.
- Collaborate on security and compliance: data residency, SSO/SAML/SCIM, regulatory standards.
- Work with engineering on API design and enterprise integration patterns.
- Define and track metrics measuring enterprise engagement, retention, and workflow depth.

## Must-Have Requirements

- 4 to 8 years of product management experience in enterprise SaaS or B2B AI.
- Demonstrable experience with on-device or edge AI, or IT fleet management.
- Strong understanding of enterprise authentication and Microsoft ecosystem (Entra ID, Intune, Azure AD, SSO, SAML, SCIM).
- Track record running structured product experiments using data for roadmap decisions.
- Direct experience working with IT admin and enterprise buyer personas.
- Technical comfort with APIs, security, compliance, and multi-tenant SaaS architecture.
- Computer Science or Electrical Engineering background preferred.

## Nice-to-Have Qualifications

- HITL workflows or agentic AI product design experience.
- Data residency, sovereign cloud, or on-premise enterprise AI deployment exposure.
- MDM/UEM platform familiarity (Jamf, Intune, or equivalent).
- AI products in regulated industries (BFSI, healthcare, government).
- Combined technical and business or GTM background.
