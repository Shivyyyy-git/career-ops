## About Chanakya

Chanakya is Sarvam's library of "replicable, deployable AI product components built on top of the Sarvam AI stack." Specific atoms include:
- Wargaming simulator
- Geospatial visualiser
- Document ingestion UI
- Ontology viewer
- MCP server
- Natural language access layers

Chanakya targets defence, government, and enterprise clients where on-premises and air-gapped constraints, structured discovery with domain experts, and high-stakes accountability are table stakes.

## Role Overview

This seat owns the Chanakya atom library product roadmap. It sits at the intersection of client feedback, engineering capability, and commercial viability. The emphasis is on decision ownership over coordination: deciding what gets built, why, and in what form — not just running standups.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own the Chanakya atom library product roadmap.
- Translate field learnings from deployments into precise product requirements.
- Conduct structured discovery with defence and enterprise clients.
- Prioritize ruthlessly and define what gets cut.
- Define and track product metrics across atom deployments.
- Write PRDs, specs, and decision logs.
- Coordinate engineering sprints and manage stakeholder communication.
