## Key Responsibilities

**Product Accountability**
Responsible and accountable for the product's value and viability, including profit and loss. Formulate and achieve Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for identified problems to solve. Drive strategy-aligned solutions; measure outcomes.

**Vision and Strategy**
Co-create, own, and evangelize the product vision, strategy, and roadmap. Align product objectives with the product portfolio and business goals. Collaborate cross-functionally.

**Market and User Engagement**
Conduct user research and competitive analysis. Engage the team with users and stakeholders through continuous research and direct interactions. Guide toward priority solutions; apply analytical skills; adopt innovative approaches.

**Collaboration and Teamwork**
Work side-by-side with cross-functional (business, engineering, experience, and delivery) team members to achieve KPI outcomes. Promote outcomes-over-output model. Build empowered teams exhibiting collective ownership.

**Continuous Improvement**
Remove obstacles. Promote rapid learning and adaptation. Drive innovation and process improvement. Spread knowledge and best practices.
