## 15. Research Notes (why each choice was made)

Source-grounded rationale for the choices in this file. Built from LinkedIn Talent Blog, Cultivated Culture (Belcak), Justin Welsh, Self Made Millennial (Mann), Andy Foote, Aakash G, Teal, Jobscan, Pragmatic Engineer, plus 2026 LinkedIn algorithm research (semantic search / 360Brew).

- **Headline weight:** Headline is indexed at ~5x other fields in recruiter search → frontload target title + AI-PM semantic neighbors.
- **About hook:** Desktop cuts at ~300 chars, mobile at ~200 — first 3 lines are the highest-leverage real estate on the entire profile.
- **Skills overhaul (2024):** Skill Assessments removed; only endorsed skills count for ranking; top-3 are pinned.
- **Open to Work:** Public green ring lifts InMails ~40–52% but biases senior recruiters; Recruiters-Only gets ~3x InMail acceptance lift without the desperation signal.
- **Activity:** Posters in last 3 months are 45% more likely to accept InMails. Comments > posts for job-hunters.
- **AI PM bar (2026):** ML literacy NOT ML expertise; ship-vs-demo signal; eval discipline. Calibrated technical claims ("fluent across eval-loop, don't train models") preempt the #1 hiring-manager skepticism.
- **Keyword stuffing penalty:** 2026 LinkedIn semantic search penalizes raw keyword repetition; reward semantic neighbors woven naturally.
- **Profile edits re-index profile** in recruiter search → small weekly edits = persistent visibility.
